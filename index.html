Six author positions, earned by contribution

Every project has six author positions. Each position comes with a defined role, deliverables and a guideline share of the logged work. You apply for a position, deliver its work, and the team confirms the final author order in writing at internal review. If someone contributes more, they move up. Nobody can buy a position.
