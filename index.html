What the training covers

Ten modules across the 12 days, built for researchers in large language models and 19 other areas. Sessions run live online, scheduled to suit your time zone.

    10 open multimodal research projects

    Ten projects across ten domains, each fusing two or more data modalities, each aimed only at a Q1 SCI journal. Every project has six open seats, one per contribution role, mapped to the CRediT taxonomy that IEEE, Elsevier, Springer and Wiley use. Seat numbers name the role, not the final author position. Author order is agreed in writing at internal review, based on each member's logged work.

    All 60 seats are open. Fees are discussed when you enquire. Each team picks its Q1 SCI journal at kickoff, after checking scope and the current quartile.
